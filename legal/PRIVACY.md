# Kash Privacy Policy

**Version 1 · Effective 11 November 2026 · Testing version**

Kash is a personal money manager made by T.I. It reads the M-Pesa, bank and investment
messages on your phone to show you where your money goes. This policy explains, in plain
language, what Kash does with your information. You must agree to it before using Kash.

## 1. The short version
- Kash reads your money-related SMS **on your phone** to track your spending.
- Your information **stays on your phone**. Kash has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no
  analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.
- You can delete everything at any time by clearing Kash's storage or uninstalling it.

## 2. What Kash reads
With your permission, Kash reads the SMS messages on your phone:
- **Messages from M-Pesa, banks, and savings or investment providers** are read to find
  transactions (amounts, balances, dates, who you paid or who paid you, and reference codes).
- **All other messages** are checked briefly, only to decide whether they are money messages
  or scams. Kash does not store their content.
- **Possible scam messages:** Kash keeps only the sender's name or number and the reason it
  was flagged, so it can warn you.

## 3. What Kash stores, and where
Kash stores the following **only on your phone**, in app-private storage that other apps
cannot read:
- the transactions it found, and the balances, categories and savings figures worked out from them;
- your answers (for example, that a payment was "Food"), your savings check-ins and your settings;
- account numbers that appear in your bank messages, so it can recognise transfers between
  your own accounts.

Encrypting this storage is being added during the testing period. Until then, please keep your
phone protected with a screen lock.

## 4. What leaves your phone
**Nothing.** In this version, Kash does not upload, send or share any of your information with
anyone, including the developer. It uses the internet for nothing except, in future, features
you will be asked to turn on first (such as an encrypted backup).

## 5. Permissions Kash asks for
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read and receive SMS | To find your transactions as they arrive |
| Notifications | For "What was this?" questions and your monthly summary |
| Fingerprint / face / PIN | To lock Kash. Android checks it; Kash never sees your fingerprint or face |

You can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. Kash will keep working with what it already has.

## 6. How long your information is kept
Until you delete it. Uninstalling Kash, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings,
permanently deletes everything Kash stored.

## 7. Your rights
Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to be informed about how your
data is used, to access it, to correct it, to object to its processing and to have it deleted.
Because your information never leaves your phone, you control all of it directly: you can see
it in the app, correct any category and delete everything at any time.
If you have a concern, contact the developer (section 11). You can also complain to the
Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

## 8. Security
Kash locks with your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN, blocks screenshots of its screens and
hides its content in the recent-apps view. Messages from ordinary phone numbers are never
treated as real money, however genuine they look.

## 9. Age
Kash is meant for people aged 18 and over.

## 10. Testing version
This is a test build. It may have mistakes: a balance or category could be wrong. Always
check important figures against your M-Pesa or bank statements. Kash gives information,
not financial advice.

## 11. Changes and contact
If this policy changes, Kash will show you the new version and ask you to agree again before
you continue.
